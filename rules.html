Members’ Code & Principles

These principles bind every approved member of the SJB Association. They complement the association’s operational rules for savings, elections, and content.

Document: SJB Members’ Code & Principles Version: 1.0 · 2026 Applies to: All approved members
Plain-language purpose. This code sets the standard of conduct for members in savings groups, elections, forums, events, and offline association activity. Breaches may lead to suspension or removal after fair review by admins.

1. Membership & identity

  1. Membership is by application and admin approval. Providing false information is grounds for rejection or later removal.
  2. Alumni status must reflect verifiable association records. Misrepresentation of class (alumni / non-alumni) is a serious breach.
  3. Each member is responsible for keeping their profile contact details accurate.
  4. Accounts are personal. Sharing login credentials, or allowing another person to vote or transact as you, is prohibited.

2. Mutual respect & communication

  1. Treat fellow members, admins, and the public with dignity — online and in person.
  2. Harassment, hate speech, threats, or discriminatory abuse will not be tolerated.
  3. Forum posts, job listings, and announcements must be truthful and relevant to the association’s purposes.
  4. Do not publish another member’s private contact or financial details without clear consent.

3. Savings groups & money

  1. Join fees and monthly contributions must be paid only through approved channels (e.g. Flutterwave flows on this site).
  2. Members must not solicit informal “side” collections that bypass association records.
  3. Withdrawal requests must state a clear purpose; approval is not automatic and may require verification that funds were disbursed.
  4. Attempting to falsify payment references or pressure admins to mark fees paid without settlement is misconduct.

4. Elections & leadership

  1. Only approved, fee-paid members may vote when an election is open.
  2. Candidates must campaign honestly; vote-buying, intimidation, or spreading false claims about opponents is forbidden.
  3. Leadership is stewardship. Office holders must put the association’s interest above personal gain.
  4. Election results and promotions follow the published process; interfering with systems or records is a grave offence.

5. Use of association platforms

  1. Job posts must be genuine opportunities; scams or misleading listings will be removed and may lead to sanctions.
  2. Do not use association channels for unlawful activity or commercial spam unrelated to member benefit.
  3. Respect intellectual property: do not upload content you do not have the right to share.
  4. Report security issues or abuse to admins promptly rather than exploiting them.

6. Privacy & data

  1. The association processes membership data to run approvals, savings, elections, and communications.
  2. Members should not scrape or bulk-export other members’ data for external use.
  3. Newsletter subscribers may unsubscribe by contacting the admin; members should not add others’ emails without consent.

7. Enforcement

  1. Admins may suspend accounts pending review. Permanent deletion of accounts is a Super Admin action.
  2. Members may raise concerns in writing to the admin contact listed on the site.
  3. Sanctions are proportionate: warning, temporary suspension, removal from a savings group, or association expulsion.
  4. This code may be updated; material changes will be announced through official association channels.

8. Acknowledgement

By holding an approved membership, you agree to follow this code together with any specific rules published for savings groups, elections, and events. Questions about interpretation should be directed to association admins before acting in doubt.
