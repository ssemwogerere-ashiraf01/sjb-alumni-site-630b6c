Who we are

Built on the same roll our founders signed

The SJB Association exists to keep members connected long after graduation — through mutual savings, mentorship, job referrals, regional chapters, and a say in who leads the association next. Membership is by approval: every application is reviewed by an admin before an account becomes active, and alumni status is verified against our records.
