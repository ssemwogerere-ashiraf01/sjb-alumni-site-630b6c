About the Association

St. Josephine Bakhita Alumni & Members Association

A chartered community of alumni and affiliated members who save together, vote together, and show up for one another — long after the classroom.

Vision

A lasting network of mutual support

We envision an association where every member — alumni and non-alumni alike — can rely on a transparent, democratic, and financially responsible community that advances opportunity across generations.

Mission

Connect, empower, and govern fairly

To keep members connected through mutual savings, mentorship, job referrals, regional chapters, and open leadership elections — with membership by approval and alumni status verified against our records.

Promise

Accountability you can see

Fees, savings contributions, and election results live in systems members can inspect. Critical changes require Super Admin oversight; day-to-day approvals stay with trusted admins.

Our story

Built on the same roll our founders signed

The SJB Association grew from a simple need: graduates and friends of the community wanted a dependable way to stay linked — not only for reunions, but for practical solidarity. Mutual savings groups, job tips, and fair elections for executive roles became the backbone of that idea.

Today the association operates as a membership body. Applications are reviewed by an admin before an account is activated. Alumni status is checked against our records; non-alumni may join under a separate membership class and fee. Once approved and fee-paid, members can join savings groups, stand or vote in elections, post on the job board, and take part in forums and events.

We are named in honour of St. Josephine Bakhita — a witness to dignity, resilience, and care for others. That spirit shapes how we treat one another in governance, money, and everyday conversation.

Membership

How joining works

1. Register Create an account with your email, name, and membership class (alumni or non-alumni).
2. Admin review An admin verifies your details. Pending accounts cannot access member-only tools until approved.
3. Membership fee Pay the published fee (alumni / non-alumni rates) securely. Your profile is marked paid only after verification.
4. Full access Join savings groups, vote in elections, use the forum, job board, and event registration.

Full conduct rules are published in our Members’ Code & Principles (readable online and downloadable as a protected PDF).

Governance

How decisions are made

Day-to-day member approvals and content creation are handled by association admins. Sensitive actions — permanent account deletion, role elevation, publishing or deleting critical content, savings activations, withdrawals, and closing elections — are reserved for the Super Admin.

Executive leadership is chosen through scheduled elections open to approved members. Winners may be promoted to leadership roles when an election is formally closed.
